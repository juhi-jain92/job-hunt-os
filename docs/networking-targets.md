# Networking Targets — AI × Adtech Overlap

Where Juhi stands out most: companies whose core problem is **supply–demand
optimization, auctions, attribution, or ad operations enablement, now being rebuilt
with AI**. That intersection is rare — most adtech PMs haven't shipped agentic
workflows, and most AI PMs have never run an auction, a yield model, or a $400M
programmatic P&L. Researched 2026-08-27, zero API spend (public web + free
Greenhouse boards).

## Top-5 digests (today's outreach set)

### 1 · Scope3 — the thesis match
Pivoted from carbon measurement to an **agentic advertising platform**: AI agents
that plan, buy, and govern programmatic campaigns, replacing the DSP workflow
layer. Launched at their Landscape conference (Mar 2026); "Brand Standards" product
replaces legacy brand-safety vendors. Publishers are publicly skeptical — which
means they need product people who've *operated* the systems being replaced.
Hiring: engineering-heavy right now (Agentic Marketplace Engineering), not on
Greenhouse — careers via site/LinkedIn.
**Her angle:** she's shipped agentic workflows with evals/guardrails/HITL *and* run
the programmatic stack their agents automate. The observation to open with: what
"Brand Standards"-style deterministic governance looks like from the sell side —
she enforced GDPR/quality policy across a $400M supply business.
**Who:** product lead / GTM-product hybrid under O'Kelley's orbit; small company, a
warm-ish note lands with founders.

### 2 · tvScientific — the attribution match
Self-serve **outcome-based CTV DSP**: app installs, purchases, leads attributed to
TV exposure; incrementality testing built in. Series B ($25.5M, 2025), positioning
as the performance-TV leader while CTV spend passes $50B in 2026.
**Her angle:** IP-to-household offline attribution she productized at Alphonso is
the primitive their measurement rests on; plus supply-side CTV (River OS, 100M+ LG
TVs) — she's been on the *other side* of the inventory they buy.
**Who:** VP Product / measurement PM. Not on Greenhouse — LinkedIn search.

### 3 · Topsort — the infrastructure match
**Auction infrastructure as an API** for marketplaces and retailers: sponsored
listings, display, in-store, AI optimization, cookieless. Small (~$28.5M raised),
11 open roles, none PM — either no PM org yet or it's founder-led product:
both are openings for a senior product person to define the function.
**Her angle:** the pitch writes itself — "the auction, yield, and quality systems
your customers get via your API are the ones I built in-house: first OEM
home-screen monetization ($16M ARR), supply-demand matching, IVT filtering."
**Who:** founders (Regina Ye, CEO) or head of eng; HM-direct with a product
observation, per the decision flow.

### 4 · Moloco — the hiring-signal play
Performance-ML ads platform expanded into **Moloco Commerce Media** (retail media
for marketplaces). **10 PM openings today** including *Director of PM, Ads Quality
& Performance — Commerce Media* and *GPM, Dynamic Product Ads*.
**Her angle:** Ads Quality & Performance is a described-by-the-JD version of her
inventory-quality ML ($4M, +20% margin) and bid-propensity work. This one goes
through the **referral lane** too — first-degree contacts at Moloco are likely
(check after CSV ingest); pair the application with a warm path.
**Who:** the hiring Director/VP for Commerce Media product.

### 5 · Kevel — the ops-enablement match
API-native **Retail Media Cloud**; 2026 momentum: Adobe AEP real-time integration,
ADvendio partnership explicitly framed as "agentic operations" (automating
trafficking, forecasting, targeting, billing), industry award for retail-media
infrastructure. Not on Greenhouse.
**Her angle:** trafficking/forecasting/ops automation is her Segment Portal and
supply-diagnostics story (2,500+ tags, 35% fewer tickets) — and they're marketing
the agentic-ops future she's already shipped internally.
**Who:** product leadership for Retail Media Cloud; LinkedIn search.
